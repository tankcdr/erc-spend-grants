# Neighbor citations

Use these in the ERC Rationale. Do not put them in `requires`.

- **ERC-7710** standardizes `redeemDelegations` and leaves `_permissionContexts` opaque. Granting a delegation is out of scope. https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/blob/master/ERCS/erc-7710.md
- **ERC-7715** is wallet JSON-RPC `wallet_requestExecutionPermissions`. https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/blob/master/ERCS/erc-7715.md
- **ERC-8226** is a regulated one-asset grant with venue-agnostic `canExecute` and `MandateReason` codes. https://github.com/ethereum/ERCs/blob/master/ERCS/erc-8226.md
- **ERC-8312** meters remaining of an opaque `capabilityRoot`; it does not enforce. https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/erc-8312-bounded-agent-actions/28851
